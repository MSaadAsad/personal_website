Naya Naqsha lets you redraw Pakistan’s provinces from divisions, districts, or tehsils, then see what your map implies for population, education, living standards, and political representation.

[Draw your map](/naqsha)

The data comes from the [Pakistan Bureau of Statistics](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/result-excel/), [Election Commission of Pakistan](https://www.ecp.gov.pk/), and [Data Darbar by Adaad](https://darbar.adaad.org/). Administrative boundaries are adapted from [pkmapr / OCHA](https://github.com/abdullahumer1101/pkmapr).
